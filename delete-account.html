Delete Your Freya Account

Freya - Daily Tarot Readings
Developer: Asuz Software

You can request deletion of your Freya account and associated account data even if you no longer have the app installed.

The easiest way is to send an account-deletion request to Asuz Software using the button below.

Request Account Deletion

If the button does not open your email app, email asuzsoftware@gmail.com with the subject Freya Account Deletion Request.

What to include in your request

Please include enough information for us to locate and verify the Freya account you want deleted:

  • If your Freya account is linked to Google, include the Google email address you used with Freya.
  • If you used Freya as a guest and you know your Freya User ID, include that ID.
  • Do not send passwords, payment card numbers, API keys, or other sensitive credentials.

What happens next

  1. We review the request and may contact you if additional information is needed to verify account ownership.
  2. After verification, we process deletion of the Freya account and associated account data that we control.
  3. We may retain limited information where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal, accounting, or regulatory obligations.

What data is deleted

Account deletion is intended to remove the Freya account and associated data controlled by Asuz Software, including account-linked profile and reading data where applicable.

Device-local information may already have been removed if the app was uninstalled or its storage was cleared.

Google Play subscriptions

Deleting your Freya account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. If you have an active subscription, manage or cancel it separately through Google Play.

Delete from inside the app

If you still have access to Freya, you can also use the in-app Delete Account option from the account/profile area.

Privacy

For more information about how Freya handles information, see the Freya Privacy Policy.
