Privacy Policy

Freya - Daily Tarot Readings
Developer: Asuz Software
Effective date: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Asuz Software ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use Freya - Daily Tarot Readings ("Freya" or the "App").

Freya is intended for users aged 18 and over.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, Freya may process the following categories of information:

  • Account and authentication information: a Firebase user identifier and, if you choose to link a Google account, information provided through Google sign-in such as your account identifier and basic account details made available by Google.
  • Age and profile information: information you provide during onboarding, including date-of-birth information used to apply the App's 18+ requirement, language preference, and other profile or reading preferences you choose to provide.
  • Reading inputs: questions, names, dates, preferences, or other text you voluntarily submit for a reading.
  • Reading results: generated reading content and related metadata needed to display, save, restore, or enforce reading availability and cooldown rules.
  • Coffee reading images: images you choose to submit for a coffee reading are processed for that reading. Freya is designed not to durably retain the raw coffee image after the reading workflow is completed.
  • Purchase and entitlement information: subscription product, entitlement, transaction, and purchase-status information processed through Google Play and RevenueCat.
  • Security and integrity information: device/app integrity and authentication signals used through services such as Firebase App Check and Google Play Integrity to protect Freya and its backend services.
  • Technical request information: network and server request information that may be processed as necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and protect the service.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • provide and personalize readings;
  • authenticate users and preserve account state;
  • verify Premium access and restore purchases;
  • save or restore Current and Saved Readings when applicable;
  • apply reading availability and cooldown rules;
  • prevent abuse, fraud, unauthorized access, and excessive automated requests;
  • maintain the security and reliability of Freya; and
  • respond to support, privacy, and account-deletion requests.

3. Service Providers

Freya uses third-party service providers that may process information on our behalf, including:

  • Google Firebase Authentication for account authentication;
  • Firebase App Check / Google Play Integrity for application and request integrity protection;
  • Google Sign-In when you choose to link a Google account;
  • Google Play Billing for Android subscriptions and purchases;
  • RevenueCat for subscription entitlement and purchase-status management; and
  • our backend service providers that operate the server-side systems required to generate and deliver readings.

Freya does not place OpenAI secret credentials in the Android application. Reading-generation requests that require AI processing are handled through our server-side systems.

4. Analytics, Advertising, and Session Replay

At the time of this policy, Freya does not include Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or a session-replay/screen-recording SDK. Freya is not designed to record your screen or replay your in-app sessions.

If our data practices materially change, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when the change becomes applicable.

5. Purchases and Subscriptions

Android purchases are processed through Google Play. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. Subscription and entitlement status may be processed by Google Play and RevenueCat. Subscription cancellation is managed through Google Play.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App, maintain account and purchase functionality, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and enforce applicable agreements.

Some reading information may also be stored locally on your device so that Current or Saved Readings can be displayed again. Removing the App or clearing application storage may remove device-local data.

Certain purchase records may continue to be retained by Google Play, RevenueCat, or other payment providers according to their own legal and accounting obligations even after a Freya account is deleted.

7. Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your Freya account from within the App. We also provide an external account-deletion page for users who cannot access the App.

Account deletion does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. You must manage or cancel your subscription separately through Google Play.

External deletion page: Delete your Freya account.

8. Data Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information, including authenticated server access, application-integrity checks, access controls, and encrypted network connections where supported by the relevant service.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. International Processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your country of residence. Where applicable, those providers are responsible for handling information under their contractual and legal obligations.

10. Users Under 18

Freya is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not intend Freya for children or minors as account holders. If you believe a person under 18 has created an account contrary to our requirements, contact us so we can review the request.

11. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability of personal information. To make a privacy request, contact us using the email address below.

12. Changes to This Policy
